READING TOOLS

A question-led documentary overview. The companion audit records evidence and verification limits.

This record applies to the revised question-led overview, edition 2.0. It replaces the audit of the broader first edition. The accompanying register contains 47 review units: 38 body paragraphs and nine nonheader table rows. These are not counts of independently verified empirical claims. Thirteen cited works were checked for identity, relevant evidence, and interpretive limits.

What was checked

#

The review examined the dated regional records, the meanings attributed to Indigenous governance sources, the description of the one implemented practice, and the proposed research process. A separate structural review tested whether each question follows from the preceding discussion. The final text distinguishes documentary facts, principles, interpretations, methodological proposals, and unresolved matters.

The first review addressed regional and research-ethics evidence. A second addressed the core concepts and the mapping account. A third examined the cumulative argument. The authoring pass then incorporated the corrections and checked the revised passages. This is an internal research audit. It is not external peer review, an ethics determination, an assessment of a project's compliance, or community endorsement.

How the scope changed

#

The paper now asks how the East Coast space sector could support Indigenous data sovereignty and what would need to be established to assess that support. It retains a short timeline and follows six linked questions. Four parallel applications and the catalogue of governance and technical arrangements have been removed. The remaining practice reference is used to make questions concrete, with its contextual limits stated.

The revised argument includes data generated by other institutions about Indigenous Peoples and territories. It identifies relevant Indigenous governance processes in the development of assessment criteria. The proposed evidence holders include both authorities and participants, without treating the list as a determination of representation.

Limits that remain

#

No local interviews, community consultations, inspections, permission tests, or outcome evaluations were conducted. The framework and suggested evidence are provisional. A public record can identify a commitment or a process without establishing how it operates. The participant account used as a precedent does not determine an appropriate Mi'kmaw arrangement.

The regional timeline retains the 27 October 2026 documentary cutoff. Sources were rechecked on 29 October for the revision. Later developments are outside that bounded record. The review was purposive and does not establish exhaustive coverage.

Mi'kmaw Ethics Watch and institutional requirements for the desk review and any subsequent research remain to be confirmed. No approval or exemption determination was obtained. The paper preserves this unresolved status; this audit does not authorize research activity or publication.
