A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Welcome to your little room.

Think of this as a miniature floor plan you can play with.

  1. Build your space. Choose Build room, then draw floor sections on the grid. Add a door and windows by clicking an outside wall.
  2. Add the fun bits. Choose Furnish and drag a piece in, or tap to add it. Move it around; select it to rotate or remove it.
  3. Find your flow. Try the feng shui-inspired arrangement, then move anything to make it your own.

Your room is saved in this browser. Export a backup to move it to another device.
