A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Welcome to your little room.

Think of this as a miniature floor plan you can play with.

  1. Build your space. Drag a corner to adjust your room. Hold a wall for curve controls. Choose Build to add doors, windows, or wall corners.
  2. Add the fun bits. Choose Furnish and drag a piece in, or tap to add it. Move it around. Select it to reveal its handles and delete button; hold it for more options.
  3. Find your flow. Try the feng shui-inspired arrangement, then move anything to make it your own.

Your room is saved in this browser. Save an editable PNG or a JSON backup to share it or move it to another device.

YOUR ROOM, YOUR SHAPE

Trace a floor plan.

Use an image as a guide. Place points along the inside of your walls. Finishing replaces the room outline, keeps your furniture, and moves existing doors and windows to the nearest new wall. You can undo the change.
