A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Welcome to your little room.

Think of this as a miniature floor plan you can play with.

  1. Build your space. Drag corners to reshape the room. Double-tap an exterior or interior wall to add a point; hold a point for its delete menu. Hold a wall for curve and point options. In Build, choose Add an interior wall, then drag or tap its two ends.
  2. Add the fun bits. Choose Furnish and drag a piece in, or tap to add it. Double-tap a piece’s edge to add a point, then drag it; hold a point for its delete menu. Hold a piece or choose Options for shape, size, color and more.
  3. Find your flow. Try the feng shui-inspired arrangement, then move anything to make it your own.

Your room is saved in this browser. Save an editable PNG or a JSON backup to share it or move it to another device.
