READING TOOLS

A critical narrative review and position paper. The companion audit records verification limits.

This record documents source verification and an internal critical review of the final paper’s 21 references. It distinguishes reported findings, interpretations, and policy recommendations, and records the evidence and limitations supporting each claim group. The internal review considered measurement validity, the case for instructor discretion, and academic presentation. It does not represent external journal peer review.

What was checked

#

The review compared claims with primary publications, author manuscripts, official webpages, and publisher or institutional bibliographic records. It checked author order and initials, publication year, titles, proceedings or journal information, page ranges, identifiers, sample sizes, effect sizes, comparison groups, and the distinction between findings and interpretation. Publication-year citations follow the published issue, rather than a later archive upload or an earlier online-first date.

For 19 of the 21 entries, full primary article or report text, or the complete official webpage, was examined. Arendasy et al. (2016) and Messick (1981) were checked against publisher or official abstracts and bibliographic records. Their use is confined to claims those records explicitly support. The audit checks how the paper represents the cited evidence; it does not independently reanalyse the datasets or every study included in a meta-analysis.

Final review decisions

#
  • Practice testing, an extended practice examination, and a full mock examination are defined separately. General retrieval-practice findings are not treated as estimates of the effect of a timed simulation.
  • The recommendation states its educational premise and allows relevant prerequisites, varied preparation, and legitimate instructor judgments about unnecessary mock-exam features. It does not require an analogue of every assessed question.
  • Direct computing evidence is identified as introductory or novice programming research. Extension to other CS subjects is explicitly a reasoned application. Voluntary uptake in Fossati and the already-prepared comparison groups in Smith are distinguished from availability effects.
  • LSAT estimates are labelled as adjusted overall comparisons. Statistical contrasts and subgroup limits are stated.
  • Study descriptions retain the verified experimental-arm distinctions, outcome boundaries, and observational limits recorded below. The Bloom examples remain illustrative. The search approach and two abstract-based checks are disclosed.
  • All 21 references have corresponding uses in the paper. Author names, dates, titles, journal or proceedings details, identifiers, and source locations were checked. Table numbering and cross-references were reconciled, and the rendered documents were visually reviewed.

Interpretive limits

#

The final paper presents a reasoned policy recommendation. The reviewed studies do not establish a universal duty to distribute a complete practice paper or full mock, or an inherent learning benefit from withholding representative preparation. The recommendation therefore retains flexibility about form, relevant prerequisites, and substantive novelty. Judging a particular course requires assessment-specific evidence. Source verification supports faithful representation of the cited literature and cannot remove the limitations of the underlying studies.
