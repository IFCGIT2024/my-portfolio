A bit about
this collection.

My interests tend to meet at the same place: understanding a complicated idea, then making it easier to explore. Sometimes that becomes a paper. Sometimes it becomes a course or a small tool.

This site brings that work together. The study material is open to use, the experiments are here to try, and the code is on GitHub.
